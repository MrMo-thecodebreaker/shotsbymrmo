HEY, I’M MO

Come as you are.
That’s the whole idea.

I’m a portrait photographer serving Vacaville, Sacramento, and the surrounding areas. I’m drawn to photographs that feel candid and timeless, and to the personalities that make every session different.

My approach is simple: keep things relaxed, make room for a little fun, and create images that feel true to the person in front of the camera. Whether it’s a solo portrait, a couple’s session, or a creative concept, we’ll shape the experience together.

I’m always learning, and every person I photograph brings a new perspective. That connection is what keeps me coming back to the camera.
